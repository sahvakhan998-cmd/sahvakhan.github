INDIA'S TALENT WAR — PREMIUM WEBSITE
======================================

This package is a redesigned, responsive static website for India's Talent War.

OPEN:
- index.html in a modern browser.

PAGES:
- Home
- About
- Auditions
- Categories
- Judges
- Gallery
- News
- Contact
- Registration

PHOTO HANDLING:
- All supplied project photos are bundled locally under assets/photos/.
- Gallery includes 94 local images and supports click-to-expand lightbox.
- No remote Unsplash image URLs are used.
- Font Awesome and Google Fonts are loaded from CDN when internet is available; the site still works with fallback fonts/icons styling.

IMPORTANT:
- Registration and contact forms are front-end demo forms. Connect them to your backend/API before production.
- Audition dates, judge names and contact details are sample/demo content and should be replaced with official information.
